<h1 id="about-heading">Hey, I'm Sascha.</h1>
<p class="research-lead">I'm a Senior Machine Learning Engineer at NVIDIA, where my research focuses on <strong>tabular foundation models</strong>.</p>
<div class="research-about-story">
<p>I'm part of the team behind <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23001">Kumo Tabular</a> and the open-source <a href="https://github.com/NVIDIA/structured-data-models">Structured Data Models</a> library. I've always wanted to do research with impact. Research that people can use and build on. I enjoy working on ambitious ideas with people who are just as excited about them as I am. With tabular foundation models, there's still so much to figure out, and the chance to help shape what comes next is incredibly exciting.</p>
<p>My interest in tabular data began during my PhD at the University of Mannheim, where I developed methods for training decision trees and ensembles with gradient descent. I later joined TU Clausthal, where my research broadened to include tabular foundation models.</p>
